# Reference-based myth machine illustrations — 2026-09-08

## Intent and provenance

Created with the built-in ImageGen tool at the project owner's explicit request. The owner's original `image.png` is a semantic reference: personal records, multiple AI/alternate selves, an oracle, human choice, and the experience-to-record loop. Its third-party collage figures and brand logos were not reused in the delivered illustrations.

The concept is the author's own response to LifeLog, not an account of a book the author has read and not an assertion that LifeLog determines human destiny. The short factual description of LifeLog is supported by the [2003 DARPA solicitation, hosted by the US Department of Defense](https://www.esd.whs.mil/Portals/54/Documents/FOID/Reading%20Room/DARPA/23-F-1033_LifeLog_05-06-2003.pdf). The generative worlds and personal-record mechanism are explicitly marked as a concept, not present-day product functionality.

## Delivered assets

- `assets/concept/myth-agency-loop-v1.png` — 1086 × 1448, four illustrated stages and a return arrow. Four Japanese labels checked visually; native HTML captions provide the detailed alternative explanation.
- `assets/concept/myth-possible-worlds-v1.png` — 1536 × 1024, underlying mechanism and multiple experience-worlds. The first world is an illustrative coastal-observatory motif; this is not a captured game screen.

Source outputs remain in the generator's original output directory; the project consumes copies saved in its own asset directory. No existing illustrations were overwritten or deleted. No fallback CLI or local diffusion model was used.
