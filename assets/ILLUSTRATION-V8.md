## Shared rendering rules

- Late-2000s to early-2010s Japanese commercial bishoujo-game illustration.
- Approximately six-head character proportion measured from the top of the head to the chin, matching the two approved full-body references: youthful large-eyed face, natural torso and leg length, and non-spindly limbs. Do not compress the body to five heads or stretch it to fashion-model proportions.
- Clean cool-colored line art, large deliberate shapes, sparse hard-edged cel shadows.
- Hair uses large white and bright-cyan reflection blocks. Eyes use a saturated base, amber/cyan reflected-light rings, and sharp white catchlights.
- No painterly paper grain, pseudo-watercolor wash, photoreal texture, 3D render, excessive micro-detail, or generic AI gloss.
- Character sprites use a genuine transparent background and show the complete figure down to both shoes.
- Existing poses are not invariants. Each expression may use a natural relaxed gesture; only identity, costume, props, six-head balance, line work, and coloring are locked.
- Backgrounds use the same clean line economy and cel-painted color grouping, but contain no foreground character unless the cue is an event CG.
- No generated text, logos, signatures, or watermarks.
