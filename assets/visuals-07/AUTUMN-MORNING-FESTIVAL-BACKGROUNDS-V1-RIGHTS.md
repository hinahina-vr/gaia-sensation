- Production date: 2026-08-20
- Production method: OpenAI built-in ImageGen (`image_gen`)
- Use case: `illustration-story` and `precise-object-edit`
- External source assets: none
- Third-party reference images: none
- Project references: existing GAIA SENSEWARE venue backgrounds and `assets/references/amane-selected-style-v8.png`
- Post-processing: none. Every repository PNG is copied byte-for-byte from its built-in ImageGen output.
- Runtime scope: current 396-step contest-v10 story backgrounds only; event CG, data views and architecture diagrams are unchanged.

## Common art direction

All eleven backgrounds were regenerated as one coherent seaside university-festival world. The shared setting is a clear early-autumn Saturday morning with a vivid blue sky, cool white daylight, ocean air, blue/white/warm-gold handmade decorations and subtle green-to-gold foliage. Entrances, halls, booth aisles and the coastal exit contain varied anonymous visitors, student groups, families and volunteers rather than empty architecture. The selected Amane reference is used only for fine linework, controlled cel shading, airy highlights and the polished 2020s bishoujo-game finish; no named character is copied into a background.

Every prompt excludes sunset, dusk, orange or pink exterior light, readable text, logos, watermarks, UI, photorealism, 3D rendering, generic glossy concept-art texture, named characters and malformed or cloned crowds. The lower 22 percent stays relatively calm for the dialogue band, and focal content remains usable in the centered mobile crop.

## Per-asset prompt set

1. Entrance: repaint the approved coastal entrance as a pleasant 09:30 early-autumn morning and add 20–30 arriving attendees plus registration staff.
2. B Hall: retain the upper-escalator viewpoint, replace sunset with morning ocean light and populate the exhibition floor with roughly 100–150 small visitors.
3. Canonical booth: retain the student-built five-surface projection concept, repaint the surrounding hall as morning and add visible aisle activity while keeping the story-sprite area usable.
4. MAP provenance: preserve the canonical booth and people; replace only the five-surface projection with a textless data-provenance map and measured observation paths.
5. GX ancient ocean: preserve the canonical booth and people; replace only projection content with primordial rain, mineral seas, currents and microbial points.
6. GX breathing points: preserve the canonical booth and people; replace only projection content with cyan microbial pulses and restrained golden coevolution filaments.
7. GX temperature anomaly: preserve the canonical booth and people; replace only projection content with a textless global anomaly map and observation traces.
8. GX gateway: preserve the canonical booth and people; replace only projection content with one Earth and exactly ten image-only mode windows.
9. Wide conversation: create a new three-quarter booth angle with a populated aisle and an empty central sprite zone.
10. Close conversation: create a closer entrance angle with projector hardware, a modest equipment table, populated side aisles and no central anonymous figure.
11. Coast exit: repaint the night coastline as a clear late-morning autumn promenade and add 25–35 anonymous festival and local visitors.
